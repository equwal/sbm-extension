sbm

sbm keeps your bookmarks as an sbm bookmark file in this browser. Sign in to sync the file with bm on your computers, the sbm app for Android and your other browsers.

Sign in to sync

No account? Create one on the website of the server. The add-on sends your bookmarks only to this server, and only after you sign in.
